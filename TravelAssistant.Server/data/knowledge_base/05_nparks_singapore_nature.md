# Singapore Parks and Nature

source_title: NParks Singapore - Parks, Nature Reserves and Park Connectors
source_url: https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/nature
source_type: public_destination_resource


Singapore has an extensive network of parks, nature reserves, nature parks and park connectors managed by the National Parks Board (NParks). These green spaces support recreation, nature appreciation, biodiversity and outdoor activities.

## Recommended nature destinations

### Central Catchment Nature Reserve
Central Catchment Nature Reserve is Singapore's largest nature reserve. It contains more than 2,000 hectares of forest and more than 20 km of trails, including the TreeTop Walk around MacRitchie Reservoir. Park hours are generally 7am to 7pm, although TreeTop Walk hours can differ.

Good activities include:
- Hiking
- Nature walks
- Birdwatching
- Exploring forest trails
- TreeTop Walk

### Bukit Timah Nature Reserve
Bukit Timah Nature Reserve is a major primary-forest area in Singapore. It is suitable for hiking, nature walks and wildlife observation. Park hours are generally 7am to 7pm.

### Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve
Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve is a major wetland and birdwatching destination. The reserve covers mangroves, mudflats, ponds and forests and is recognised for migratory birds. Park hours are generally 7am to 7pm, with last entry at 6.30pm.

### East Coast Park
East Coast Park is an eastern coastal recreation area. It is open 24 hours and is suitable for cycling, inline skating, camping and barbecuing.

### Fort Canning Park
Fort Canning Park is a central green space combining nature and heritage. It is open 24 hours and is suitable for nature walks, arts or heritage activities and photography.

### Singapore Botanic Gardens
Singapore Botanic Gardens is a major central destination for nature, family activities, nature walks and arts or heritage experiences. Park hours are generally 5am to midnight.

### Coney Island Park
Coney Island Park is an eastern nature destination suitable for cycling, hiking and nature walks. Park hours are generally 7am to 7pm.

### Southern Ridges
The Southern Ridges form a roughly 10 km sequence of connected green spaces including Mount Faber Park, Telok Blangah Hill Park, HortPark and Kent Ridge Park. Highlights include Henderson Waves, Forest Walk, Canopy Walk and Berlayer Creek.

## Park Connector Network

Singapore's Park Connector Network links parks and nature areas through linear green corridors. Park connectors support walking, running, cycling and recreational travel while also improving ecological connectivity.

Examples of longer recreational routes include:
- North Eastern Riverine Loop
- Eastern Coastal Loop
- Northern Explorer Loop
- Central Urban Loop
- Eastern Corridor
- Western Adventure Loop

## Nature reserves

Singapore has four nature reserves:
- Central Catchment Nature Reserve
- Bukit Timah Nature Reserve
- Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve
- Labrador Nature Reserve

Nature reserves protect ecosystems and native flora and fauna. Visitors should follow posted rules and stay on designated trails.

## Visitor etiquette

Visitors should:
- Stay on designated trails.
- Follow signs and park rules.
- Avoid feeding or approaching wildlife.
- Keep noise levels low.
- Carry litter away or use designated bins.
- Apply for required permits for organised group activities or camping.
