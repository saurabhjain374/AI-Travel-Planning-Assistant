# Singapore Heritage, Museums and Culture

The National Heritage Board (NHB) is the custodian of Singapore's heritage. NHB manages national museums and heritage institutions, oversees heritage sites and National Monuments, and develops programmes that connect Singapore's past and present.

## Heritage institutions

Singapore's heritage institutions provide opportunities to learn about the history and cultural heritage of different communities.

### Indian Heritage Centre

The Indian Heritage Centre is located in Little India and traces the history of Indian and South Asian communities in Singapore and Southeast Asia. It includes permanent galleries covering centuries of history, trade and community, together with exhibition and activity spaces.

The centre can be combined with a visit to the historic Little India precinct.

### Malay Heritage Centre

The Malay Heritage Centre is located at Istana Kampong Gelam and is a key heritage institution for Singapore's Malay community. It provides cultural and historical context for the Kampong Gelam area and Singapore's Malay heritage.

### Sun Yat Sen Nanyang Memorial Hall

Sun Yat Sen Nanyang Memorial Hall is a heritage institution that explores the history and connections associated with Sun Yat Sen and the Chinese revolutionary movement in the Nanyang region.

## Museums and heritage resources

NHB operates or oversees a broad network of museums, heritage interpretative centres and community heritage institutions. Its heritage resources include:
- National museums
- Community heritage institutions
- National Monuments
- Historic sites
- Heritage trails
- Intangible cultural heritage
- The National Collection

Roots.sg is a heritage resource portal associated with NHB and provides access to digitised heritage information and artefacts.

## Cultural travel in Singapore

For travellers interested in history and culture, useful areas and experiences include:
- Little India and the Indian Heritage Centre
- Kampong Gelam and the Malay Heritage Centre
- Historic districts and heritage trails
- National museums and heritage institutions
- National Monuments and historic sites

Heritage attractions can be combined with food, walking and neighbourhood experiences to create culturally focused itineraries.

## Travel-planning guidance

When recommending museums or heritage attractions, verify current opening hours, admission requirements and temporary closures from the relevant official institution before travel.
