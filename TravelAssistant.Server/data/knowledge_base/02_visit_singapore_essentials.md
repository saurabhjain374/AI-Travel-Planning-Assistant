## Planning Your Singapore Trip

Singapore is a compact destination where many major attractions can be reached using public transportation. Travellers can combine MRT journeys with walking and bus services.

When planning a day, visitors should consider travel time between neighbourhoods and attractions rather than scheduling too many distant locations together.

Singapore's tropical climate means that heat, humidity, and rainfall should be considered when planning outdoor activities.

## Getting Around

### MRT

The MRT is one of the most convenient ways for visitors to travel around Singapore. It connects many major tourist areas and residential districts.

Visitors can use MRT stations to reach areas such as Marina Bay, Chinatown, Little India, Bugis, Orchard, and other major destinations.

### Public Buses

Singapore's public bus network provides extensive coverage and can complement the MRT.

Buses are useful when an attraction or hotel is not located directly next to an MRT station.

### Taxis and Ride-Hailing

Taxis and ride-hailing services provide convenient alternatives when travelling with luggage, during late hours, or when a destination is less convenient to reach by public transport.

### Walking

Walking can be a good way to explore compact cultural districts such as Chinatown, Little India, and Kampong Glam.

However, visitors should consider Singapore's heat and humidity when planning long walking routes.

## Money and Payments

Singapore uses the Singapore dollar (SGD).

Credit cards and electronic payment methods are widely accepted at many hotels, restaurants, shopping centres, and attractions.

Travellers should still carry a suitable payment option for smaller establishments or locations where a particular payment method may not be accepted.

Current exchange rates should always be obtained from a current currency service rather than relying on a static travel document.

## Weather and Climate

Singapore has a tropical climate throughout the year.

Visitors should expect warm and humid conditions and should be prepared for rainfall.

Outdoor sightseeing should include practical considerations such as:

- Carrying drinking water
- Using sun protection
- Taking breaks during long walks
- Carrying an umbrella or rain protection
- Checking current weather conditions before outdoor activities

The current weather should be retrieved from a live weather service when creating a weather-aware itinerary.

## Connectivity

Travellers can use mobile connectivity options such as local SIM cards, eSIMs, and international roaming depending on their requirements.

Wi-Fi is available in many hotels, shopping centres, restaurants, and public locations.

Travellers should check their mobile provider's roaming terms before departure.

## Food and Dining

Singapore offers a broad range of dining experiences.

Visitors can explore:

- Hawker centres
- Food courts
- Local restaurants
- Chinese cuisine
- Malay cuisine
- Indian cuisine
- Peranakan cuisine
- International restaurants

Hawker centres are particularly useful for travellers who want to experience a variety of local dishes in one location.

## Cultural Considerations

Singapore is a multicultural destination with Chinese, Malay, Indian, and other cultural influences.

Visitors should respect local customs and follow posted rules when visiting religious and cultural sites.

When entering temples, mosques, or other religious places, travellers should follow the dress and behaviour guidelines provided at the site.

## Practical Daily Planning

A practical Singapore itinerary should avoid placing too many geographically distant attractions in the same time period.

A good approach is to group attractions by area.

For example:

### Central Singapore

Marina Bay, Gardens by the Bay, Singapore River, museums, and nearby attractions can be combined.

### Cultural Singapore

Chinatown, Little India, and Kampong Glam can be explored as cultural and food-focused experiences.

### Leisure Singapore

Sentosa, beaches, gardens, and nature attractions can be used for a more relaxed day.

## Weather-Aware Planning

When current weather information indicates heavy rain, travellers should consider replacing or rearranging outdoor activities.

Examples of indoor alternatives include:

- Museums
- Cloud Forest
- Flower Dome
- Shopping centres
- Indoor food experiences
- Cultural attractions

When weather conditions are suitable, outdoor options can include:

- Gardens
- Cultural neighbourhood walks
- Sentosa
- East Coast
- Nature attractions

The assistant should use current weather information rather than this document when deciding whether today's conditions are suitable for outdoor activities.
