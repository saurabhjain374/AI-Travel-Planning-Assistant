# Entering Singapore

Singapore's Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) publishes the official entry requirements for travellers.

## Travel documents

Travellers entering Singapore must hold a valid individually issued travel document. Travellers other than Singapore passport holders generally need a passport with at least six months of validity.

Entry requirements can vary according to nationality, travel document and traveller circumstances.

## Visa requirements

Travellers holding passports or travel documents from visa-required countries or places must obtain a valid Singapore entry visa before travelling to seek entry into Singapore.

A Singapore entry visa is pre-entry permission to travel to Singapore and seek entry. A valid visa does not itself guarantee entry; admission and the duration of stay are determined at the checkpoint.

The official ICA visa-requirements page lists the countries and travel documents that require a visa.

## SG Arrival Card

Travellers generally need to submit the SG Arrival Card within three days, including the day of arrival, before arriving in Singapore.

The SG Arrival Card is not a visa.

The SG Arrival Card submission is free when completed through the official ICA SG Arrival Card e-Service or the MyICA Mobile application. Travellers should use official ICA channels and be cautious of unofficial websites that charge fees for this free submission.

Some travellers are exempt from SG Arrival Card submission, including certain passengers transiting or transferring through Singapore without seeking immigration clearance and specified Singapore residents entering through land checkpoints.

## Visa-Free Transit Facility

Eligible nationals of certain countries or regions may qualify for Singapore's Visa-Free Transit Facility for short transit stays, subject to the applicable conditions.

Eligibility can depend on nationality, travel document, onward travel and other requirements. Travellers should check the current ICA requirements before relying on the facility.

## Other entry considerations

Short-term travellers should:
- Have a valid travel document.
- Check whether a visa is required.
- Submit the SG Arrival Card when required.
- Follow immigration clearance procedures.
- Have evidence of onward travel where applicable.
- Ensure they do not overstay the granted Visit Pass.

## Important freshness rule

Immigration and visa information is time-sensitive. The information in this knowledge document is intended to support grounded answers, but travellers should always verify current requirements directly with Singapore ICA before travelling.
