# Gardens by the Bay

Gardens by the Bay is a major horticultural attraction in Singapore's Marina Bay area. The Gardens combine large outdoor gardens with conservatories, vertical gardens, plant collections, visitor facilities and seasonal experiences.

## Visitor planning

The official Gardens by the Bay visitor information provides:
- Opening hours and closure information
- Directions and getting-there information
- Parking information
- Gardens maps
- Visitor information guides
- Amenities and services

The official visitor page should be checked before a trip because attraction hours, closures, events and access arrangements can change.

## Main experience areas

Gardens by the Bay includes major attractions and garden areas such as:
- Flower Dome
- Cloud Forest
- Supertree Grove
- Outdoor gardens
- Waterfront areas
- Seasonal and special experiences

The Cooled Conservatories feature plants and environments from different parts of the world. Supertree Grove is known for its large vertical garden structures and evening illumination experiences.

## Planning a visit

Gardens by the Bay can be included in itineraries focused on:
- Architecture and urban landscapes
- Gardens and horticulture
- Family activities
- Photography
- Marina Bay sightseeing
- Evening experiences

The attraction is particularly convenient to combine with other Marina Bay destinations.

## Accessibility and visitor information

Gardens by the Bay provides visitor guides and social stories designed to help visitors understand what to expect during a visit. These resources cover general visitor experiences and selected attractions.

## Current information

Some experiences, dining locations, show timings, pedestrian routes and access arrangements may change temporarily because of events, maintenance or construction. The official Gardens by the Bay website should therefore be used to verify current details before travel.
