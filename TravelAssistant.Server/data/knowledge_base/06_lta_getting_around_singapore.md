# Getting Around Singapore

Singapore has an integrated transport network covering rail, buses, taxis, private-hire cars, walking and cycling. The Land Transport Authority (LTA) provides official information for planning journeys and understanding public transport.

## Public transport

Singapore's public transport system consists primarily of MRT/LRT rail services and an extensive public bus network.

### Rail network

Singapore's rail network has more than 140 stations across six MRT lines, forming a roughly 200 km system. There are also more than 40 stations across two LRT lines.

The MRT/LRT network is one of the main ways to travel between central Singapore, residential areas, tourist attractions and other parts of the island.

### Bus network

Public buses complement the rail system and reach business districts, residential neighbourhoods and tourist attractions. Trunk buses provide longer routes between neighbourhoods, while feeder services connect MRT stations and bus interchanges with surrounding areas.

## Planning a journey

LTA's journey-planning tools can be used to find routes, calculate fares and estimate travel times. The MyTransport.SG app provides information such as train operating times, station exits, public bus services and bus arrival information.

For a travel itinerary, public transport is generally a useful option for connecting major attractions without relying on private vehicles.

## Paying for public transport

Depending on the service and prevailing rules, travellers can use contactless bank cards, supported mobile wallets and stored-value or concession cards. Fare calculation and transfer rules are provided by LTA and the Public Transport Council.

Travellers should check current fares and payment methods before travelling because transport policies and fares can change.

## Taxis and private-hire cars

Taxis and private-hire cars provide door-to-door transport. Taxis can be hailed where permitted, used at designated taxi stands or booked through mobile applications. Private-hire services can also be booked through mobile applications.

Taxi fares can include:
- Flag-down fare
- Distance and time-based charges
- Surcharges
- Booking fees

Travellers should check the fare shown by the service provider and current LTA information.

## Walking and cycling

Singapore supports walking and cycling through sheltered walkways, park connectors and other active-mobility infrastructure. Cycling can be useful for selected recreational routes and park-connector journeys.

Visitors using cycling or personal mobility infrastructure should follow current rules and safety guidance.

## Driving

Singapore has an extensive road network. Drivers should follow prevailing road rules, signage and Electronic Road Pricing (ERP) requirements where applicable.

## Travel-planning guidance

For itinerary generation:
- Prefer MRT and buses for many cross-city journeys.
- Consider walking between nearby attractions.
- Consider cycling for suitable park and recreational routes.
- Use taxis or private-hire cars when door-to-door convenience is important.
- Always check current LTA journey information for exact routes, fares and travel times.
